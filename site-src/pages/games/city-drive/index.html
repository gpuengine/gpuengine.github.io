City Drive

An endless city, generated as you drive. Downtown towers give way to suburbs, parks and the waterfront, with live traffic on every street.

Play City Drive

Needs a browser with WebGPU: recent Chrome, Edge or Safari, or Firefox on Windows. A laptop with a dedicated or modern integrated GPU is recommended.

How it's built

City Drive is plain JavaScript and WGSL with no engine or library underneath. The world is a road graph that grows around the car; a Web Worker builds the buildings, trees and street furniture for each new block so the main thread only has to draw. Shadows come from a sun shadow map, and a post-processing pass finishes each frame.

The articles take each of these systems apart in detail.
